Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

ABESsystems (registered brand name: ABES Systems) is a sole proprietorship run by Ben Abesera in Los Angeles, California. This policy explains what we collect through abessystems.com, email and text messages, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • What you send us through the contact form or by email: your name, email address, business name, your message and, if you choose to give it, your mobile number.
  • Whether you agreed to receive text messages, and when.
  • Messages you send to or receive from our business phone number.

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to you, to schedule and run the work you ask for, and to send the messages you agreed to. We do not sell your information.

Text messages and mobile numbers

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We share your number only with the service providers that deliver our messages (such as our SMS provider, Twilio), and only so they can deliver them.

You can stop texts at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. See our Terms & SMS terms for details.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the business, for example email hosting, form handling (Formspree), scheduling (Calendly), payments (Stripe) and text messaging (Twilio). They process information only on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while we are talking or working together, and for as long as the law requires afterwards. Ask us and we'll delete it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing ben@abessystems.com. California residents have these rights under the CCPA.
